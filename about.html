How I approach design

Complexity is our burden, not the user's

Every product has layers of complexity underneath it. The design job is to absorb that so users never feel it. For them, it should just work.

From chaos to clarity

I can work through a mess. Figure out what actually matters, what is worth solving, and keep the team focused on the outcome even when the starting point is unclear.

Craft with a deadline

High attention to detail matters. So does shipping. The tension between the two is real and I have learned to navigate it, knowing when to push for quality and when good enough is exactly right.

Everyone owns the experience

Being the designer doesn't mean I own the user. Engineers, PMs, and designers all bring different strengths to the table, and the best products come from everyone feeling responsible for the experience.
